About

Built to feel trustworthy, useful, and easy to maintain

This project started as a simple password generator and now presents itself like a production utility: clear hierarchy, responsive layout, privacy-focused messaging, and enough supporting content to stand up as a real website.

What changed

  • Modern responsive layout with header, sidebar, and content areas.
  • Dedicated pages for policy, support, and common questions.
  • Ad-ready spaces that do not crowd the primary workflow.
  • Accessible controls, dark mode, and cleaner feedback states.

Why it matters

Utility pages often fail on presentation and trust. The revised version solves that by making the interface look intentional, giving users enough context to understand what the tool does, and keeping the core action obvious from the first screen.
