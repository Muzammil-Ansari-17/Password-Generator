Common questions, answered clearly

These answers cover privacy, the generator workflow, and why the site includes reserved ad and trust content. The goal is to make the page feel complete and easy to scan.

Quick facts

What users should know

  • Generator output stays local to the browser.
  • The site uses semantic sections and structured data.
  • Ad placements are planned instead of bolted on.
Does the generator store my password?

No. Passwords are generated locally in your browser and are not sent to a server.

Can I use the site on mobile?

Yes. The layout is built mobile-first and scales cleanly across phones, tablets, and desktops.

Why are ad spaces included?

Reserved placements make the site monetization-ready without pushing ads into the generator workflow.

Does the redesign affect password generation?

No. The logic stays the same. Only the UI, content, metadata, and supporting interactions were expanded.

What should I do if I forget a password?

Use a password manager, rotate credentials where needed, and regenerate strong passwords for new accounts.
