Questions

What people usually want to know

Does the generator store passwords?

No. Passwords are generated locally in your browser and are not sent to a server.

Can I use the site on mobile?

Yes. The layout is built mobile-first and scales cleanly across phones, tablets, and desktops.

Why are there ad spaces?

The page includes reserved placements for banner, in-content, sidebar, and footer ads without crowding the main task.

How do I make a stronger password?

Use a longer length and enable all character sets. The generator ensures at least one character from each selected set.

Can I trust the clipboard button?

Yes. The button only copies the password you generated on the page and gives visible feedback when the action succeeds.
