How this site handles data

The generator is designed to work locally in the browser. It does not require an account or submission of personal data to generate passwords.

Important points

Overview

Privacy policy

This project is built as a static site. The password generator runs in the browser and does not intentionally transmit generated passwords to a server.

Information we collect

The site does not ask you to create an account. If you click a contact link, the information you choose to send is handled by your mail client or GitHub, not by a custom backend on this site.

Local storage

Theme preference may be stored locally so the site can remember whether you prefer light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is not used to identify you.

Ad and analytics services

If advertising is enabled in the future, Google AdSense or similar services may use cookies or measurement technologies under their own policies. The site layout reserves space for those placements without crowding the generator.

Contact messages

Messages sent through mail links or GitHub are used only to respond to your inquiry or review source-related questions.
