Privacy policy

How this site handles data

The generator is designed to work locally in the browser. It does not require an account or submission of personal data to generate passwords.

At a glance

Important points

  • No intentional password logging.
  • Theme preference may be stored locally.
  • Ad services, if enabled, would be handled by third parties.

Privacy policy

This project is built as a static site. The password generator runs in the browser and does not intentionally transmit generated passwords to a server.

Information we collect

The site does not ask you to create an account. If you click a contact link, the information you choose to send is handled by your mail client or GitHub, not by a custom backend on this site.

Local storage

Theme preference may be stored locally so the site can remember whether you prefer light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is not used to identify you.

Third-party services

If advertising or analytics are added later, they may be provided by third parties with their own policies. Those services should only be enabled after the appropriate review and account setup.

Disclaimer

The site is provided as-is for educational and utility use. You are responsible for following your own password policy and device security standards.

Policy summary

Short version

  • Password generation stays on your device.
  • Only basic UI preferences may be saved locally.
  • No account is required to use the tool.
  • Contact requests go through your own mail or GitHub client.
